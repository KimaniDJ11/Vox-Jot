# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

Vox Jot welcomes people of different backgrounds, identities, abilities,
experience levels, and viewpoints. Contributors and maintainers commit to making
participation respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, specific, and kind
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, licenses, and responsible disclosure
- Accept correction and make a good-faith effort to repair harm
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users
- Assume different levels of technical experience and explain without belittling

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing private recordings, transcripts, credentials, or identifying logs
- Deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or misleading claims of official
  status
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `kimani@iriedinamik.org`. Include links or
screenshots needed to understand the incident, but minimize unrelated personal
data. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Reports will be handled as privately and consistently as practical. This policy
applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing Vox Jot
or Irie Dinamik.
