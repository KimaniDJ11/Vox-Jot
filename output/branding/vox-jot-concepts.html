Vox Jot Brand Study

Four directions for a calmer, more trustworthy voice brand

These concepts lean into the current product story: private voice dictation, local processing, and lightweight everyday use. They also bridge the gap between the new premium onboarding UI and the old inherited glove icon.

What the concepts keep

Every option aims to feel legible at app-icon size, flexible enough for macOS, Windows, web, and tray uses, and distinct from generic microphone-only marks.

  • Voice + text implied together
  • Friendly but not cartoonish
  • Good fit for local/private positioning
  • Compatible with both Vox Jot blue and Dinamik purple families
Direction A

Quiet Wave

Soft utility-first mark. A voice waveform resolves into a notebook stroke, so the icon feels both spoken and written.
